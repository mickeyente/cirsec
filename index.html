CyberSafety 2026 Campaign Overview

What is the Cirsec Cybersecurity Awareness Initiative?

The Cirsec Cybersecurity Awareness Initiative 2026 is a month-long cybersecurity education and public awareness campaign taking place throughout October 2026 in Lagos, Nigeria.

The initiative is designed to make cybersecurity practical, understandable and relevant to organisations, employees, business owners, communities and digital users. The campaign combines physical organisational outreach with digital education, media engagement and interactive activities.

Our Approach

Rather than treating cybersecurity as an issue only for technical teams, the initiative focuses on the people and everyday behaviours that contribute to organisational security. Our goal is to help people understand cybersecurity before an incident turns into a financial, operational or reputational problem.

Why It Matters

Modern organisations depend heavily on email, cloud platforms, mobile devices, online payments, websites, software and digital communication. As this dependence increases, cybersecurity risks can affect more than technology. They can affect:

  • Business operations
  • Customer trust
  • Company information
  • Financial resources
  • Reputation
  • Business continuity
  • Employee productivity
  • Future growth

Why Your Organisation Should Participate

A cybersecurity incident does not always begin with a sophisticated technical attack. It can begin with a suspicious email, a fraudulent message, a compromised account, an unsafe password, a fake link or an employee unknowingly making the wrong decision. That is why cybersecurity awareness should extend beyond the IT department.

  • Improve employee awareness: Help employees recognise common cyber threats and suspicious activity.
  • Reduce preventable mistakes: Build awareness around phishing, social engineering, scams, account security and unsafe digital behaviour.
  • Strengthen security culture: Encourage employees to take cybersecurity seriously as part of their everyday responsibilities.
  • Support business continuity: Help employees and management understand how cybersecurity incidents can disrupt business operations.
  • Protect trust: Increase awareness of risks that can affect customer information, company reputation and business relationships.
  • Encourage better reporting: Help employees understand when suspicious activity should be reported and what actions to take.

Core Training Areas

  1. Cybersecurity Fundamentals: Common threats, impacts, everyday risks, and employee mistakes.
  2. Phishing & Social Engineering: Recognising suspicious emails, fake links, and social engineering techniques.
  3. Account & Access Security: Password security, multi-factor authentication, and safe access practices.
  4. Scams & Digital Fraud: Online scams, fraudulent communications, and suspicious payment activity.
  5. Incident Awareness & Reporting: Recognising suspicious activity, what to do, and when to report.

How The Training Works

The organisation engagement is designed as an interactive learning session, rather than a conventional lecture.

  • Awareness Session: Introduction to key concepts and practical behaviors.
  • Practical Demonstrations: Examples of common risks and warning signs.
  • Scenario-Based Learning: Working through realistic situations.
  • Interactive Discussion: Q&A tailored to the participants' work.
  • Cybersecurity Quiz: Testing understanding through interactive questions.
  • Key Takeaways: Practical recommendations employees can apply immediately.
